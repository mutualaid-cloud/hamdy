About Me

My journey with the camera began in 2010, driven by boundless passion and a dream of going as far as possible in the world of photography. Although the harsh realities of my circumstances dictated a different path and prevented some of those aspirations from being realized at the time, that dream remained alive within me. Today, I find a new opportunity in every shot to rekindle that longing and continue the journey with a more mature and steadfast perspective.

Beyond being a photographer, I am a writer. I view images and words as two sides of the same quest: sometimes my texts are born from the photographs I take, while at other times, my reflections guide how I frame a shot. For me, photography is a space for meditation; each image holds an intimate story—a narrative of its own—and the echo of the emotions that stirred within me the very instant I pressed the shutter.
