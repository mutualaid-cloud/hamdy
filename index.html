Hamdy

Photographer, Writer & Seeker of Light

My journey with the camera began in 2010, driven by boundless passion and a dream of going as far as possible in the world of photography. Although circumstances dictated a different path, that dream remained alive within me. Today, I find a new opportunity in every shot to continue with a more mature perspective.

Beyond being a photographer, I am a writer. I view images and words as two sides of the same quest. For me, photography is a space for meditation; each image holds an intimate story and the echo of emotions felt in the instant I pressed the shutter.
